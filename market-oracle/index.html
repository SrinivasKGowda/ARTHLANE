Nifty options idea from the Nifty signal and implied volatility
Educational tool, not investment advice. Signals are computed automatically from daily technicals (moving averages, RSI, MACD, ATR, breakouts, volume), headline tone, Nifty option-chain data (PCR, OI build-up, max pain, bid/ask quantity) and global cues. They can be wrong, and past patterns do not guarantee future moves. Always use a stop-loss and size positions to your risk. Publishing buy or sell calls to the public in India requires SEBI Research Analyst registration.
